The Past Arc

The Silence of Waves

Hidden deep in the cliffs of the Hidden Cloud Village, the Anitsu Clan guarded a secret older than the village itself: the Wave Style, a Kekkei Genkai that manipulates vibration itself — and the perfect counter to the Uchiha's visual jutsu. When the Uchiha leadership judged that secret an existential threat, they struck one new-moon night in a covert operation disguised as a rogue attack. In a single night, over two hundred Anitsu were silenced. Only four survived — and one of them was a boy just one year old.

ACT I

The Secret War

The Uchiha had long studied the Anitsu. They knew the cooldowns, the blind spots, the weaknesses. When the Third War ended, the order was given — and a twelve-man strike team moved under diplomatic cover.

ACT II

The Night of Broken Waves

The attack came at 3:00 AM, when Anitsu wave-sensitivity was lowest. Clan head Nayuta fell defending his family — while Nyoko, the clan's chief medic, bought four precious minutes with a forbidden final technique.

ACT III

The Prince of a Dead Clan

Raided by his cousin Nora at age one, Nanzo grew up alone under the weight of 200 dead — raised on grief, legacy, and a buried truth he would only learn at age ten. Until one stubborn girl saw through the perfect exterior to the broken boy beneath.

Power System

The Wave Style

The Anitsu Kekkei Genkai manipulates vibration itself — from shockwaves in the air to the very brain waves of a living mind. It is the natural counter to Genjutsu: a Wave Style master can shatter illusions before they take hold, and even destabilize an opponent's chakra network. But every wave has its limits.

Wave Style

Vibrational pulses that bypass skin and muscle and strike organs directly — liquefying lungs, shattering tenketsu, disrupting chakra flow. At its height, it can rupture internal organs without an external wound.

Gravity Manipulation

Nanzo's own invention — founded on his academy expertise in physics. Calculated fields of artificial gravity pin opponents like specimens. Brilliant, precise, and utterly unlike anything the clan's ancestors imagined.

Wind Style

Nanzo's second element — long and mid-range blades of compressed air, like the Massive Destroyer. Devastating at a distance, but unusable up close without killing the user too.

Pulse Reading

The Anitsu sensory art: reading shifts in the village's chakra atmosphere — tension, fear, shock. Nyoko's Final Resonance could be felt by every Anitsu across the Hidden Cloud.

The Five-Meter Dead Zone

Wave pulses cannot be emitted within five meters of the user without harming themselves. Inside that circle, a Wave Style master has no jutsu, no taijutsu, no kenjutsu — nothing but their own body.

The Cooldowns

Fifteen minutes between major Wave Style techniques. One minute of Gravity Manipulation, then thirty minutes of helplessness. Power without adaptability becomes helplessness — a lesson Karui taught the Prince the hard way.

Complete Arsenal

Nanzo's Full Power Arsenal

Every technique in the Prince's arsenal — the restored chronicles from the old records. Beyond Kage Level. Beyond World Level. Some of it, he has never once used.

Blood Exclusive Kekkei Genkai — only Anitsu Clan blood descendants possess it. Karui does not have it, despite marriage. Vibrational pulses bypass skin and strike the inside of a body directly.

Internal Organ Destruction

Wave pulses liquefy lungs, rupture hearts without an external wound, shatter spinal columns. Perfect assassination — death with no visible cause.

Brain Wave Disruption

Precise strikes destroy neural pathways, cause cerebral hemorrhage through vibration, and shut down all brain function instantly. No external injury.

Chakra Network Collapse

Destructive waves through the tenketsu cause permanent chakra pathway destruction. Victims die from internal system failure. Medical ninjutsu cannot restore shattered nodes.

The Weakness

Wave pulses cannot be emitted within five meters of the user without harming the user. Inside that circle there is no jutsu, no taijutsu, no kenjutsu — only the body. And fifteen long minutes between major techniques.

No one else has acquired these jutsu. Nanzo developed them using his academy physics expertise — devastating at range, suicidal up close. Until she taught him otherwise.

Vortex

Mid range — destroys everything near it by absorption. At short range, the user dies by absorption himself.

Massive Destroyer

Long range — wind performs destruction on impact. At short range, the user dies from the massive destroyer.

Air Absorption

Absorbs oxygen within range (~1km), killing targets. Five-meter rule: no effect within five meters of Nanzo himself.

Paralyze Sword

Discovered after marriage, with Karui's help. When the sword hits an opponent, wind pressure destroys cells at the contact point — causing permanent paralysis of the struck body part. Destroyed cells cannot be healed by conventional medical ninjutsu.

Karui's Gift: she taught him to channel wind chakra through the sword safely at close range — turning his greatest weakness into a weapon.

Wavering Wind

World Exclusive: winds form in waves. Opponents hit have their chakra stopped plus wind destruction inside the body — causing them to fly or die. Only Nanzo can perform this Wave + Wind combo.

Founded and created by Nanzo alone — not an Anitsu Clan art. Built on his academic expertise in physics, manipulating gravitational fields through chakra application.

Absolute Gravity Lock

Can lock even gods in place — they cannot move, eye genjutsu cannot move, not a single grain of sand can shift. Can stop millions of armies, monsters, Susanoo, even gods — all helpless for one full minute.

Absolute Lock Effects

When gravity is activated, NO NINJUTSU, NO GENJUTSU, NO TAIJUTSU can be performed — not a single hair can move, safe-mode orbs won't move. Complete paralysis of all physical and chakra-based action.

Invented by Nanzo — his original sealing technique and clone art, using Wave Style principles.

Sealing Jutsu: Waves Destroyer

Uses waves to destroy/seal full chakra — the target cannot use any chakra until unsealed. No jutsu, no chakra access. Effectively powerless.

God-Level Abilities

  • Wave Control: all waves within 200km are his to command — sound, electromagnetic, chakra, seismic.
  • Chakra Domination: can control ALL chakra flow within 200km — even gods' chakra.
  • Internal Destruction at Scale: can kill anyone within 200km in seconds using Wave Style internal destruction.
  • Sensory Overload: waves destroy ear balance — victims cannot stand or walk normally.
  • Destroyer Winds: Wind Style becomes apocalyptic "Destroyer Powers."
  • Wave Speed: movement at the speed of wave propagation — god-level velocity.
  • Flight: can move and fly at wave speed — he is NOT immobilized.
Indiscriminate Apocalypse: within 200km, ALL MILLIONS OF PERSONS are stuck — allies, friends, everyone. The gravity/wave control has no ally-exclusion. Once activated, no one performs ninjutsu, genjutsu, or taijutsu.
The Vulnerability Paradox: theoretically, if someone could stab his heart in Waver Mode, he would die — he is not truly immortal. Practically impossible: his god powers make him untouchable — he controls all waves, all chakra, all movement within 200km. No one can approach, no attack can reach him. Effectively invincible for the full duration. The only death is the 30-minute limit itself.

🛡️ TOP SECRET — Shibai Ōtsutsuki Descent

The Anitsu Clan descends directly from Shibai Ōtsutsuki. This is a closely guarded secret — NO ONE knows it outside the clan head. Their Wave Style Kekkei Genkai is derived from Shibai's divine abilities, explaining its god-like potential. Karui, as Nanzo's wife, may be one of the few trusted with this truth.
