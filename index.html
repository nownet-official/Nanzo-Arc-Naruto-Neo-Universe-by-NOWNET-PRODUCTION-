Naruto Neo Universe

An original novel universe set in a reborn shinobi world. Naruto Neo Universe introduces a new era — original arcs, original clans like the Anitsu, and stories built from the ground up, beginning with the Nanzo Arc: a tale of the Hidden Cloud.

NOWNET Production

NOWNET is an independent creative production team crafting original stories, novels and digital experiences. The Nanzo Arc novel edition is our first public release — written by Navaneeth Krishna — and this is only the beginning of the Neo Universe saga.
